# The Billionaires' Dashboard 2025
This project is an excel dashboard that breaks down the world of billionaires in a simple and engaging way. It highlights who the richest people are, where they come from, the industries driving their wealth and some interesting patterns behind their success.
## What the Dashboard Shows
**1. Net Worth Overview**
A combination of bar and line charts show how net worth varies across the top billionaires. It makes it easy to spot the biggest names and see the general trends.

**2. KPIs**
The dashboard includes three key metrics: **Average Net Worth, Average Age, and Total Number of Billionaires**. These figures give a quick sense of the overall landscape without digging too deep.

**3. Industry Comparison**
This section breaks down which industries produce the most billionaires. It's a great way to see where wealth is most concentrated. 

**4. Gender & Wealth Source**
You can filter the dashboard to see:
* How many billionaires are male vs female
* Who built their wealth vs who inherited theirs
This sort of adds more context to the story behind their success.

**5. Regional Trends**
The billionaires are grouped by regions, and this helps highlight which parts of the world have the most billionaires.
### Tools and Skills Used
* Data cleaning and transformation
* Pivot tables
* Pivot charts
* Slicers for interactivity
## Conclusion
This dashboard pulls together the most important details about today's billionaires and presents them in a simple and interactive format. It highlights where their wealth comes from, how it is distributed across industries and regions and common trends amongst them.
